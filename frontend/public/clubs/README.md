Club crests in this folder are stored locally and served from `/clubs/*.svg`.

The original seeded club crests and the additional matched crests are from [Jose Arroyave's football-logos repository](https://github.com/JoseArroyave/football-logos), whose repository license is included in `LICENSE.txt`. Club crests and names remain the property of their respective clubs.

The available source files were matched by club name to the OpenFootball directory. Clubs without a safe, unambiguous match use a generated shield with their initials in the interface. This keeps every club visibly represented without presenting a made-up crest as the club's official logo.
